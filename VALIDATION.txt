Astra Current Monitor v1.3.1 validation summary

- Automated unit tests cover camera queue handling, recognition, measurement
  timing, peak handling, and durable storage.
- The migrated ten-image regression set checks blue upper-row voltage and red
  lower-row current readings.

  when the window is shorter than its content.

against the physical instrument before relying on the readings.
